Process

How I work.

A predictable rhythm from first conversation to production release, designed so you always know what's happening and what's next.

01

Understand

Map the problem, the constraints, and the outcome that matters most. No code until the shape is clear.

02

Plan

Scope, architecture, and delivery sequence agreed up front. Small pieces, in the right order, with clear checkpoints.

03

Build

Ship in visible slices, not one big reveal at the end. You see progress every week, not just at the finish line.

04

Refine

Test, polish, and hand over something maintainable. Post-launch fixes are part of the engagement, not an upsell.
